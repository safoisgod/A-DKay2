General

A-D Kay Publications is a publishing house dedicated to promoting African literature, sharing stories that inspire and educate.

You can reach us via email at support@adkaypublications.com or by phone at +233 (54) 355-0766. Alternatively, use our contact form.

Author Submissions

You can submit your manuscript through our author submissions form. Ensure your manuscript is in PDF or DOC format and includes a synopsis.

We accept a wide range of genres, with a focus on African literature, including fiction, non-fiction, poetry, and children’s books.
