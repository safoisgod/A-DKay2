FAQs

General

A-D Kay Publications is a publishing house dedicated to promoting African literature, sharing stories that inspire and educate, with a focus on socio-economic, religious, and political themes from an African perspective. Learn more on our About Us page.

You can reach us via email at support@adkaypublications.com or by phone at +233 (54) 355-0766. Alternatively, use our contact form.

Our mission is to use writing as a tool for transformation, increasing awareness, promoting literacy, and encouraging education, particularly within African communities. Visit our About Us page for details.

Books and Purchasing

Use our Book Collection page to browse or search for titles. You can filter by genre, author, or keyword to find exactly what you’re looking for.

Our books are available in hardcover, paperback, and eBook formats. Check each book’s page on our Book Collection for specific availability.

Yes, we ship to many countries worldwide. Shipping costs and times vary by location—contact us via our contact form for details.

We occasionally offer signed copies during special events. Sign up for our newsletter via our contact form to stay updated on these opportunities.

The second edition of Killed by Illiteracy will be available internationally on Amazon starting March 2025. Check our Book Collection or follow us on Facebook for updates.

Author Submissions

You can submit your manuscript through our author submissions form. Ensure your manuscript is in PDF or DOC format and includes a synopsis.

We accept a wide range of genres, with a focus on African literature, including fiction, non-fiction, poetry, and children’s books. Learn more about our publishing process on our submissions page.

Yes, we provide comprehensive publishing services, including editing, book design, marketing, distribution, and author support. Explore our services on our homepage.

Careers

Check our careers page for current openings. You can also submit your resume for future opportunities via our contact form.

Partnerships

We welcome partnerships with organizations that share our vision. Please fill out our partnership form to start the conversation.

Yes, we partner with schools like Bertrace School Complex and Bediako Memorial School to integrate our books into curricula and promote literacy. Learn more about our partnerships on our About Us page.

Community and Literacy

We promote literacy by publishing educational literature, donating books to underserved schools, and supporting initiatives like the Ghana Library Authority’s Year of Literacy in 2021. Read about our mission on our About Us page.

Yes, we welcome volunteers and partners to support our literacy programs. Contact us via our contact form to learn how you can get involved.
